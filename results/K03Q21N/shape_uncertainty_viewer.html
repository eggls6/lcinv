Mean shape

Mean surface of posterior shape draws (spin-state posterior samples, each with a Laplace draw of the shape coefficients). Every draw is rotated about the spin axis (vertical, z) to best match the ensemble mean before radii are compared, because the rotational phase is a gauge freedom. Radii are measured from the centroid in units of the volume-equivalent sphere radius Req.

Caveat: these solutions spin far from their maximum-inertia axis (dashed); see the inversion notes before interpreting the shape physically.
